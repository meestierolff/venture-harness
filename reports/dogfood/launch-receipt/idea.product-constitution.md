# Launch Receipt Product Constitution

- Category: A focused web app that helps an indie SaaS founder verify essential launch readiness and publish a clear receipt of what is ready and what remains.
- Promise: Publish a clean launch receipt that accurately communicates readiness and outstanding work
- First user: An indie hacker preparing to launch a SaaS product
- Job to be done: Consolidate scattered launch requirements, proof, and remaining actions into one trustworthy readiness view
- Native product object: A launch-readiness checklist whose items include status and optional proof, rendered as a publishable receipt
- Primary journey: The founder creates a launch receipt, reviews the essential launch items, marks each item ready or remaining, attaches proof where useful, previews the result, and publishes one shareable receipt.
- Business-model boundary: free; none; price not asserted; one commitment event (The founder publishes a launch receipt.).
- First learning question: Will the target user produce published_launch_receipt before 2026-09-08?

## Truth register

- FACT — The proposed product is a web app.
- FACT — The intended user is an indie hacker about to launch a SaaS.
- FACT — The founder explicitly excludes project-management suites, generic startup dashboards, and another Venture Harness control plane.
- FACT — A monthly subscription was suggested only as a possibility.
- FOUNDER_ASSUMPTION — A focused checklist plus publishable receipt is sufficient for an initially useful outcome.
- FOUNDER_ASSUMPTION — Founders will manually enter statuses and proof without provider integrations.
- FOUNDER_ASSUMPTION — An unguessable editing link can replace user authentication for the first version.
- FOUNDER_ASSUMPTION — A free launch is appropriate until willingness to pay is observed.
- MODEL_INFERENCE — Publishing a receipt requires persisted state and a stable shareable URL.
- MODEL_INFERENCE — Real product usage is useful but not indispensable before testing the concept, so a thin MVP is appropriate.
- UNKNOWN — Which launch items users consider essential
- UNKNOWN — What forms of proof users need to attach or link
- UNKNOWN — Whether published receipts are primarily private working documents or public trust artifacts
- UNKNOWN — Whether users will pay, how much they might pay, and whether a subscription fits the usage pattern
- FIXTURE — Any sample or synthetic data must be visibly labeled at its public surface.

Truth classes are FACT, FOUNDER_ASSUMPTION, MODEL_INFERENCE, FIXTURE, EXTERNALLY_VERIFIED, UNKNOWN, and CONTRADICTORY.

Models may improve framing, prioritization, language, design, and implementation. Models may not invent provider state, users, demand, metrics, results, customers, revenue, reviews, source URLs, or testimonials.

## Scope exclusions

- Project-management features
- Generic startup dashboards
- A Venture Harness control plane
- Team collaboration
- Provider integrations
- Automated readiness verification
- Custom checklist templates
- Billing
