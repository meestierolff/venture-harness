# iOS App Store optimization

Use this playbook only when the venture activates the iOS ASO capability and
an App Store record or explicit manual-record node exists.

## Ownership and metadata

Record the canonical owner for:

- app name/title, subtitle, keyword field, short and long description;
- category and positioning;
- privacy/support/marketing URLs;
- primary language and each approved localization;
- version, release notes, subscription/purchase facts, and availability;
- screenshot sets, preview assets, and their narrative order.

Keep web product truth, store claims, pricing source, and entitlement source
consistent. Never say an Apple product, build, TestFlight state, rating, review,
install, or store availability exists until provider read-back confirms it.

## Asset system

Define one screenshot narrative per device family/localization: user problem,
smallest useful journey, verified outcome, differentiator, and next action.
Generate assets deterministically where practical, keep source templates and
safe-area/device rules versioned, and verify dimensions, order, contrast,
legibility, labels, and localization. Illustrative UI and sample data remain
labeled.

## Measurement

Normalize App Store Analytics Reports with account, fetched time, window,
timezone, dimensions, threshold/sampling limitations, and release version.
Relate discovery to product-page views, downloads/installs where available,
activation, trial/subscription outcome, retention/deletion, crash/quality, and
support burden. Do not infer retention or revenue from downloads.

Use product-page experiments only when traffic and decision value justify
them. Change one concept, declare the metric/guardrails/stopping rule first,
and preserve exposure/version context.

## Ratings and reviews

Ask for ratings only through ethical platform-compliant UX after a genuine
value moment. Never gate features, suppress negative users, manipulate timing,
buy reviews, prefill sentiment, or fabricate responses. Qualitative review
analysis must be safely classified and de-identified before entering the
learning system.

## Release boundary

The first app record may be a manual node. TestFlight upload, metadata, groups,
and supported App Store Connect operations use official transports and secure
credential refs. App Store publication is irreversible enough to require its
own authorization checkpoint; never equate upload, processing, TestFlight, or
review submission with being live.
