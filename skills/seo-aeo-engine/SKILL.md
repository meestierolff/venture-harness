# seo-aeo-engine

## Purpose

Route discovery work to the smallest relevant playbook, connect it to qualified
product outcomes, and keep every recommendation inside product truth.

## Trigger conditions

- Web technical discovery, query ownership, search performance, AEO/GEO, or ASO work.
- GSC, GA4, Bing, AI-referral, App Store, RevenueCat, or release data is available.
- A route, metadata field, sitemap, crawler rule, store listing, or discovery
  measurement changes.

## When not to use

- Offer or pricing decisions, visual identity, paid acquisition, social
  automation, or product features unrelated to discovery.
- Publication solely to increase page count, keyword coverage, or citations.

## Required inputs

- `docs/product/PRODUCT_TRUTH.md` and approved offer/pricing sources.
- The venture contract, active capabilities, relevant routes/store metadata,
  and normalized datasets with source, account, window, timezone, freshness,
  and quality limitations.
- Existing canonical owners before any new page or store asset is proposed.

## Documents to read

Always read product truth and the active plan. Then load only the relevant
resource:

- Web technical checks: `references/technical-discovery.md`
- SEO and topical ownership: `references/seo-topical-system.md`
- AEO, GEO, AI referrals, and crawler governance: `references/aeo-geo.md`
- iOS ASO and store assets: `references/aso.md`
- Data contracts, scoring, reports, and recovery: `references/measurement-and-operations.md`
- Full v0.1 wording when resolving a migration ambiguity:
  `references/seo-aeo-v0.1-preserved.md`

## Files this skill may change

`docs/growth/**`, discovery metadata and public HTML under `app/**`, truthful
structured data, internal links, sitemap/robots through the applicable approval
envelope, store metadata/assets, discovery validators, fixtures, and dated
reports. Keep each change PR-sized.

## Files this skill must not change

Approved pricing, product capabilities, testimonials, credential values,
analytics PII boundaries, unrelated product/design code, or production crawler
policy outside authorization.

## Execution steps

1. Select one mode: technical, topical, performance, recovery, GEO/AEO, or ASO.
2. Establish product-truth boundaries, locale, canonical/store owner, data
   window, freshness, and limitations.
3. Inspect existing owners and protect verified winners before proposing work.
4. Choose the smallest action: verify, monitor, repair, improve, consolidate,
   redirect, create, or reject.
5. Implement or draft one coherent change with direct answers, original value,
   intentional links/assets, and an appropriate conversion outcome.
6. Run the capability-selected source and rendered/store checks.
7. Record evidence, result, approval requirement, and monitoring date.

## Hard rules

- Product truth outranks discovery opportunity.
- Never fabricate queries, traffic, citations, prompts, expertise, ratings,
  reviews, installs, retention, or store state.
- Missing is not zero; sampled or thresholded data stays labeled.
- One material query cluster has zero or one intentional canonical owner.
- Improve or consolidate before creating a new page; no thin query variants.
- Important answers and product facts must be readable without client-only JS.
- Structured data and store metadata describe visible, verified reality.
- `llms.txt`, IndexNow receipt, rankings, citations, and prompt observations are
  not guarantees or standalone business outcomes.
- Ratings prompts remain ethical and platform-compliant.
- Publication, crawler-policy changes, redirects, and store submission remain
  authorization-envelope effects.

## Expected output

A dated, evidence-cited decision or PR-sized diff with ownership state,
data-quality notes, user/business effect, verification results, monitoring date,
and only genuine approvals or manual actions.

## Validation

Run the active discovery checks selected by `config/quality.yaml`, including
source checks and rendered raw HTML for public web changes. Validate ASO metadata
and deterministic assets for mobile changes. State every skipped live check with
the exact credential, command, and expected evidence.

## Failure behaviour

Keep unaffected work moving. Classify missing or malformed data, truth
conflicts, provider outages, absent servers, and crawler differences exactly;
never substitute fabricated output or broaden scope to create apparent progress.

## Human approval boundaries

Humans or an explicit authorization envelope gate publication, material core
page rewrites, redirects, canonicals, crawler access, broad structured-data
changes, confirmed-wedge replacement, rating prompts, and App Store submission.
