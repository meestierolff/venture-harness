# Code of conduct

Venture Harness is a technical community where direct critique and respectful
collaboration can coexist. Participation in repository discussions, issues,
reviews, and related community spaces requires this conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific about code, evidence, and tradeoffs; do not attack people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect names, pronouns, privacy, and cultural differences.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and distinguish facts from assumptions.
- Protect vulnerability details, credentials, and personal information.
- Accept a maintainer's request to pause or move a sensitive discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, retaliation, or publishing
another person's private information is not acceptable. Neither is using the
project to facilitate deceptive claims, spam, credential abuse, or unauthorized
provider effects.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct issues, contact a maintainer privately through the repository's
available private contact channel. Security vulnerabilities use
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be shared only with people needed to
review and respond, subject to legal and safety obligations.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, impose a
temporary participation limit, or permanently exclude a participant. Decisions
consider impact, pattern, intent, repair, and community safety. A person directly
involved in a report should recuse from the enforcement decision when another
maintainer is available.
