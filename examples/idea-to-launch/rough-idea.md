# SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT LIVE CUSTOMER OR PROVIDER EVIDENCE

I run a small freelance web studio. At project handoff, account details,
maintenance notes, and acceptance steps are scattered across email and docs.

Build a focused web app called Handoff Note. It should let a freelancer create
one structured client handoff, complete its essential checklist, and publish a
read-only handoff link. Charge a hypothetical EUR 9 monthly subscription with
Stripe. Keep it to one owner and one handoff flow; do not add teams, project
management, file storage, a marketplace, or automated client outreach.
