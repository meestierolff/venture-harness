# SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT LIVE CUSTOMER OR PROVIDER EVIDENCE

## Handoff Note Product Constitution

- Promise: publish one clear client handoff from a structured project-closing checklist.
- Proposition hypothesis: close a client website with one structured handoff that becomes a clear read-only client record.
- First user: a solo freelance web developer completing a client website.
- Native product object: one handoff with draft and published states.
- Primary journey: create, complete, preview, publish, and open the read-only handoff.
- Commitment boundary: a hypothetical EUR 9 monthly Stripe test subscription; no real charge.
- Success signal: `handoff_published`.
- First learning question: does the published view replace the freelancer's existing handoff document?

Models may improve the product and its presentation. They may not invent users,
provider state, payments, demand, metrics, testimonials, or delivery evidence.

Product copy and design are venture-owned. Core upgrades must preserve them.
