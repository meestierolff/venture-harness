# SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT LIVE CUSTOMER OR PROVIDER EVIDENCE

## Handoff Note

A focused web app for solo freelance web developers who need to close a client
website with one trustworthy handoff instead of scattered notes.

The canonical structured input is [launch-contract.yaml](launch-contract.yaml).
Its smallest credible product is one owner-only handoff editor and one
read-only published view. The synthetic EUR 9 monthly amount is a hypothesis,
not a live price or evidence of willingness to pay.

Proposition hypothesis: close a client website with one structured handoff that
becomes a clear read-only client record.

### Primary journey

1. Sign in and create one handoff.
2. Complete the essential sections and preview the client view.
3. Publish the handoff and open its read-only link.

### Explicitly excluded

Teams, project management, file storage, template marketplaces, and automated
client outreach.
