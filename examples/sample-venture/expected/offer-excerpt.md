## Thirty-day cash (verbatim calculator output)

See [thirty-day-cash.txt](thirty-day-cash.txt) — EUR 149/month + EUR 400
setup against EUR 320 CAC recovers acquisition cost inside 30 days
(margin 86%) **under stated assumptions**. The setup fee is doing the
work: without it, payback would exceed the 30-day target. That is why
the offer includes done-for-you historical bill import as a priced setup,
not a free onboarding step.

## Surviving weaknesses (from the attack pass)

1. Urgency unproven — latent pain may not convert to monthly payment.
2. Recoverable-spend claim (2–5%) has no systematic evidence; forbidden
   on public pages until PRODUCT_TRUTH has data (status: UNVERIFIED).
3. Dispute execution unclear (us vs them) — changes delivery cost.

## Kill criteria (illustrative)

- Fewer than 5 qualified applications after 60 days and 500+ ICP visits.
- Interviewees consistently frame this as a yearly audit, not a monthly
  service, after 10+ interviews.
