# VENTURE BRIEF — MeterMate

- Status: SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — not a real venture
- Owner: (illustrative founder)
- Last updated: 2026-07-21

> SYNTHETIC: this entire brief is invented to demonstrate the harness.

## The idea in one paragraph

Small property managers get utility bills for dozens of meters and mostly
just pay them. Billing errors, estimated readings that never get
corrected, and slow leaks hide in those PDFs for months. MeterMate reads
the bills, flags anomalies ("this water bill is 3x the seasonal norm for
this building"), and tells the manager exactly what to dispute or
inspect. Why now: bills arrive digitally almost everywhere since 2024,
and reading messy PDFs reliably stopped being a research problem.

## The customer

Property managers running 20–200 residential units across 3–15 buildings,
in-house or as a small agency. One person owns "operations costs" and
personally approves utility invoices. Not: large managers (they have
energy consultants), not single landlords (pain too infrequent).

## The pain

They pay bills they don't have time to check. When an error is found, it
is found months late by accident. BELIEF, NOT EVIDENCE: we think 2–5% of
utility spend is recoverable; we have two anecdotes from acquaintances,
zero systematic data.

## The outcome

Recovered money and prevented waste, measured: disputed amounts credited
plus consumption anomalies fixed, visible per building per month. First
useful result should be day one: upload last 12 months of bills, get the
anomaly list.

## Money

They already pay for property-management software (tens of EUR per unit
per year) and occasionally energy consultants (project fees). Hypothesis:
EUR 149/month flat up to 200 units + EUR 400 setup (historical bill
import done for them). No evidence yet on willingness to pay.

## Reachability

Regional property-manager associations, two active forums, a handful of
LinkedIn groups, trade newsletters, and the accountants who serve them.
The founder knows four property managers personally (warm start).

## Existing alternatives

Doing nothing (dominant), spreadsheets, energy consultants (expensive,
project-based), utility-cost modules in PM software (data entry, no
anomaly detection). Switching cost is low because we sit on top of bills,
not inside their workflow.

## What you already have

Founder ran ops for a 90-unit portfolio for three years (domain
knowledge). A rough PDF-parsing prototype on 40 sample bills, ~90% field
accuracy. No waitlist, no audience.

## Constraints

Solo founder, ~20 hours/week, EUR 6k budget for validation. No hardware
metering — bills only. German + English markets only.

## Open questions

- Will managers hand over bills without procurement friction?
- Is the pain urgent enough to pay monthly, or is this a yearly audit?
- Who exactly disputes the bill — us (concierge) or them?
