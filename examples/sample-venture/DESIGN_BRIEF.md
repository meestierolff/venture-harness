# DESIGN BRIEF — MeterMate

- Status: SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — not a real venture
- Owner: (illustrative founder)
- Last updated: 2026-07-21

> SYNTHETIC: this entire brief is invented to demonstrate the harness.

## How the product should feel

Precise (it reads numbers for a living), calm (it removes a worry, it
does not add a dashboard), frugal (its whole promise is saving money —
the design must not look expensive for its own sake), grounded (buildings
and pipes, not clouds and gradients).

## The emotional outcome for the visitor

"Finally — someone checks these bills, and I don't have to." Relief plus
mild vindication: they always suspected the bills were wrong.

## References — principles, not pixels

- A well-designed bank statement: tabular clarity, numbers you trust.
  Principle: typographic hierarchy does the work, not decoration.
- Industrial equipment labels: unambiguous, durable, no ornament.
  Principle: utilitarian confidence.
- A good plumber's invoice website: plain, priced, human.
  Principle: price and process stated without ceremony.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with purple gradients and floating cards —
  our customers distrust "startup gloss" attached to their money.
- Energy dashboards with gauges everywhere — we are the opposite of
  another dashboard.
- Anything resembling utility-company branding (they are the antagonist).

## Voice

Dry, specific, slightly wry. Sample: "Your water bill for Hausweg 12 is
three times its seasonal norm. Either you have a pool now, or you have a
problem."

## Constraints

Name "MeterMate" is fixed (synthetic). WCAG AA. German and English copy.
Avoid utility-brand color coding (no energy-provider yellow/violet).

## Assets on hand

None. All product visuals must be labeled illustrative interfaces built
from the real prototype's field structure — never fake screenshots
presented as real.
