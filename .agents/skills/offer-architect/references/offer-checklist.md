# Offer checklist

Every item answered, evidenced or labeled assumption, before the offer is
considered test-ready.

## Market

- [ ] Starving crowd: is the pain already being paid for, badly?
- [ ] Pain: what breaks/costs/wastes, in the customer's words?
- [ ] Purchasing power: can THIS customer spend the price?
- [ ] Targetability: can we reach them without guessing?
- [ ] Urgency: why act this quarter, not someday?
- [ ] Frequency: how often does the pain recur?

## Promise

- [ ] One-sentence offer in the required structure.
- [ ] Dream outcome stated as the customer would say it.
- [ ] Perceived likelihood: why would they believe us?
- [ ] Proof on hand vs proof missing (listed separately).
- [ ] Time delay to first value; day-one win defined.
- [ ] Effort and sacrifice inventoried; done-for-you opportunities marked.

## Money

- [ ] Setup fee decision (and annual waiver decision).
- [ ] Day-one upsell.
- [ ] Conditional guarantee with plain conditions.
- [ ] Thirty-day cash: calculator run, output pasted, payback ≤ target.
- [ ] CAC, delivery cost, contribution margin assumptions stated.

## Position

- [ ] Competitive separation vs named alternatives (incl. "do nothing").
- [ ] Initial wedge; expansion path; moat hypothesis.
- [ ] Kill criteria written before launch.
