## Purpose

Outreach playbook and log. Every message a human sends is drafted with
value first, personalised from a real observation, capped at two follow-ups,
and approved by a named human. Agents never send anything.

## Message principles

- Lead with something useful to them, not a pitch.
- Reference a real, specific observation (their post, their situation).
- One clear, low-effort ask.
- No mass generic messages; no scraping in violation of platform rules.
