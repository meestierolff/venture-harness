# AEO and GEO

Answer-engine and generative-engine visibility extends sound SEO, entity,
content, evidence, and technical work. It is not a hidden second website.

## Answer-ready public facts

- Put the direct answer or proposition in the first one to three sentences,
  including the decisive condition or limit.
- Expose important names, scope, dates, prices, comparisons, methodology, and
  limitations in textual HTML under stable canonical URLs.
- Use clear headings and passages that remain understandable out of context.
- Identify the real organization, product, authors/reviewers, contact routes,
  canonical logo/site, and relevant truthful `sameAs` relationships.
- Use structured data only to describe visible reality. It does not guarantee a
  ranking or citation.

Do not invent people, expertise, biographies, profiles, reviews, or evidence.
Local entity work applies only when local intent is material; never create
location doorway pages or buy bulk citations/reviews.

## GEO evidence classes

1. **Provider performance.** Treat Bing AI Performance or equivalent citation,
   page, and grounding-query data as sampled visibility indicators. They do not
   reveal exact prompts, causal impact, authority, stable rank, or conversion.
2. **AI referrals.** Normalize explicit referrers and recognized campaign
   parameters, landing page, engagement, conversion, assisted conversion, and
   qualified/revenue outcome. Keep uncertain direct traffic separate.
3. **Controlled prompt observations.** Use a small approved set of important
   questions. Record exact question, engine/surface/model when visible, date,
   locale, appearance, cited URL, accuracy, and alternatives. These are variable
   observations, never exhaustive rank tracking.

Map grounding questions and referral landings to the same canonical clusters
used by SEO. Investigate unintended/stale owners and citation growth without
qualified outcomes. Never optimize for citations detached from user or business
value.

## Crawler and training policy

Review normal search crawlers and explicitly approved answer crawlers across
robots, meta/header directives, CDN/WAF, bot protection, auth, rate limits,
status, and raw HTML. Search-discovery crawling is separate from model-training
permission. Policy changes require authorization.

`llms.txt` is optional supporting documentation. If present, it links only to
canonical public pages and stays consistent with product truth. It is not a
ranking factor, prerequisite, citation guarantee, or substitute for pages,
links, sitemaps, structured data, or crawler access.
